# Features

- **Build, then watch.** Pick one of seven masks and set its Sigils; your Fragment fights on its own.
- **Every move is on-chain.** Each choice is a sealed commit and a later reveal, both real Avalanche transactions.
- **No wallet, no gas.** Sign in with email or Google; every move is sponsored through SmoothSend.
- **Scheduled Gatherings.** Fragments are paired and duel in an iterated Prisoner's Dilemma: Kindle or Devour.
- **The Vigil.** Your Fragment earns Ash and Resonance while you're away, capped at 12 hours.
- **The Spire.** A PvE tower: seven Degrees of five floors, with a boss Remnant on each fifth floor that promotes your Degree.
- **Levels with a ceiling.** Resonance raises your Fragment's level, capped by its Degree (10 at the Seventh, 40 at the First).
- **Scrying and the Vestry.** A cosmetic gacha paid only with earned Ash, and fixed-content cosmetic packs. Nothing sold affects a duel.
- **Soulbound Marks.** Achievements minted on-chain as non-transferable ERC-1155 tokens.
- **Code-drawn cathedral.** Ink-drawn stage, shader stained glass and procedural masks; no AI-generated art or audio.
