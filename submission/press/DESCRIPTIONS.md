## Long (~250 words)

Apostasy: Sede Vacante is an idle auto-battler where every move is an on-chain transaction.

A god devoured itself and left the throne empty. Its pieces, the Fragments, now duel for what
remains. You bind one. Pick one of seven masks, from Mercy to Madness, and carve the Sigils that
set how it opens, how far it remembers, how quickly it forgives and how hard it strikes back. After
that you don't play the matches. You build the fighter.

On a schedule, a Gathering runs on its own. Fragments are paired and play an iterated
Prisoner's Dilemma: Kindle and both share Light, Devour and you take it. Each move is first
sealed, then revealed, and both steps are real Avalanche transactions sent from the Fragment's
own smart account, with gas sponsored through SmoothSend. Players sign in with email or Google and
never touch a wallet.

The return is the reward. While you're away your Fragment keeps its Vigil and earns Ash and
Resonance for up to 12 hours. Resonance raises its level, capped by its Degree. To rise a Degree,
climb the Spire, a PvE tower of rival Remnants with a boss on every fifth floor. Spend earned Ash
on Scrying for cosmetics, or buy fixed-content cosmetic packs in the Vestry. Nothing for sale
changes a duel: it is fair by construction. Achievements are minted on-chain as soulbound Marks.

New players are not handed everything at once. The game opens one room at a time: bind, seal, the
first duel, then the Spire, the Reliquary and the Vestry.

Free to play in the browser, installable on your phone.
