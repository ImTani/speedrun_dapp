## Surface

**The Gathering.** This is the replay of one Meeting between two masked Fragments: 5 turns, each a Kindle or Devour, already settled on-chain.
- **Mode:** Experience.
- **Phone-first,** 390×844.
- **Skippable:** tap hastens, hold rushes, Skip jumps to the result, and the Skip-animations setting persists.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The Gathering is a *place*, not a panel: two lit windows set side by side in one cathedral wall. The exchange is told only through light, glass, candles and stone. It refuses the card-battle HUD: pills, chips, pop-up banners, cartoon tokens and floating panels.

**OWN-WORLD.**
- **Walls:** a night ashlar wall. Each window sits in a deep splayed embrasure with a stone sill, and a colonnette stands between them.
- **Glass:** leaded jewel glass using the locked shader.
- **Votives:** wrought-iron racks holding red glass votive cups.
- **Plaques:** engraved brass plaques screwed to the stone.
- **Floor:** dark worn marble in perspective. It receives each window's coloured light as caustic pools, with inlaid brass roundels and brass inscription letters.
- **Type:** Grenze Gotisch for inscriptions, Spectral italic for liturgy, Alegreya Sans SC for engraved small caps. Nothing is rounded, pill-shaped or floating.

**STORY.** The player watches two masked Fragments meet and reads each turn at a glance:
- **The window's glass:** warm and flaring means Kindle, bleeding crimson means Devour.
- **The candles** that light, one per point of Light.
- **The light pools** on the floor, which grow, meet or are drained.

They leave knowing the Light they gathered and where they stand tonight.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.**
- **The wall fills the top half.** A carved title band runs along the top. Below it, the two windows (148×207) sit in embrasures with a colonnette between them and a stringcourse beneath.
- **Under each sill** is a votive rack (3×8 cups) above a brass plaque. The plaque carries the name, the Aspect, the enamelled choice and the Light count.
- **The floor fills the bottom half,** with the two light pools, a central brass inscription and five inlaid turn roundels.
- **Controls are fittings:** an iron ring (back) at top-left, and a brass hourglass (skip animations) and a "Pass" plate (skip) at top-right.

**SIGNATURE INTERACTION.** Each turn runs: hush, then glass reveal, then light exchange, then candles light one by one with embers drifting along the light.
- **Kindle–Kindle:** the pools meet at the centre.
- **Devour:** embers are drawn from the victim's side, its candles gutter and smoke, and its glass dims and cracks.

Motion is stepped by real causes (flame, glass, light), never squash-and-stretch cartoon physics.

**FORM.** The composition is the user's pin: "one wall, side by side". It was chosen over the facing-transepts option. Direction lineage: world seed key `80aa973e` (stained-glass world); this surface is a pinned composition, so no surface roll was made.
